Frequently asked questions

Everything you wanted to know (but were too busy grinding to ask).

Is TaskHero free to use?+
Yes — TaskHero is free and open source (MIT licence). A school simply runs it on one of its own computers.
Where is my data stored?+
On your school's own TaskHero server, in a database file that belongs to your school only. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and no data is sent to third parties or external services.
Who is TaskHero for?+
IB MYP students, teachers and school administrators. Students hand in work, teachers grade it, and school admins manage accounts.
How do teachers get an account?+
Teachers can sign up with the School ID, but their account must be approved by the school administrator before they can log in. Admins can also create accounts directly.
What subjects are supported?+
All eight MYP subject groups: Language & Literature, Language Acquisition, Individuals & Societies, Sciences, Mathematics, Arts, Physical & Health Education and Design — plus "Other".
How do streaks work?+
Earn XP (by handing in work or finishing quests) on consecutive days to keep your streak. A longer streak multiplies the XP you earn — up to 5× at 30 days. XP is calculated by the server, so it cannot be edited in the browser.
