Frequently asked questions

Everything you wanted to know (but were too busy grinding to ask).

Is TaskHero free to use?+
Yes — TaskHero is 100% free. Everything runs in your browser, so there are no subscriptions, no hidden fees, and no ads.
Is my data safe?+
Your progress is stored locally on your device using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server. You can clear your data anytime from your browser settings.
Who is TaskHero for?+
TaskHero is designed specifically for IB MYP Year 1–4 students, but anyone who wants a fun, gamified way to stay organized can use it.
Can teachers use TaskHero too?+
Absolutely. Teachers can create classes, post assignments, and manage students — all from the same clean dashboard.
What subjects are supported?+
Math, Science, Individuals & Societies (IS), Language & Literature, Language Acquisition, Arts, Design, and Physical & Health Education. You can also create custom subjects.
How do streaks work?+
Complete at least one task every day to keep your streak alive. Each day of streak adds a multiplier to your XP — up to 5x at a 30-day streak.
